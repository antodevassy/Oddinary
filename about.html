About Oddinary - Word Imposter Game

Oddinary - Word Imposter Game is a fast-paced social deduction and word association party game designed for groups of friends, family gatherings, team events, and game nights. Playable on a single shared phone with zero setup or accounts required.

The Concept

Everyone receives a secret word. One or more players receive something slightly different (or no word at all) — they are the Oddinaries. Players take turns giving subtle one-sentence clues without giving the secret away. Afterwards, everyone investigates, debates suspicious clues, and votes to uncover the Oddinary.

Single-Device Play

Pass one phone around the circle. No need for everyone to download apps or create accounts.

Stealth & Classic Modes

In Stealth Mode, the Oddinary doesn't know they have the odd word. In Classic Mode, they bluff from clue one.

Secret Alliance

Play with 2+ Oddinaries who secretly recognize each other and coordinate their deceptive clues.

Instant & Free

Play directly in your browser with 3 to 30 players anytime.

Game Modes Explained

Stealth Mode

In Stealth Mode, the Oddinary receives a word that is closely related to the common word (e.g. Coffee vs Tea, or Burger vs Pizza). The Oddinary does not know their word is different. They describe their word sincerely until they realize someone else's clues don't quite match up.

Classic Mode

When Stealth Mode is turned off, the Oddinary receives no word and is directly notified: "YOU ARE THE ODDINARY". They must bluff on the spot and deduce the common word from other players' clues.

Scoring Rules & Championship

In-app Secret Voting automatically tracks player scores across multiple rounds to crown the Oddinary Champion:

DETECTIVES (Common Word Players)

  • +15 Points for each correctly identified Oddinary.
  • 0 Points for an incorrect vote.

ODDINARY (The Imposters)

  • 0 Points if caught (received highest votes or tied for highest).
  • +10 Points if survived the round without receiving the most votes.
  • +20 Points if completely undetected (zero votes received).
  • +5 Bonus Points for Betrayal (an Oddinary voting against another Oddinary).
