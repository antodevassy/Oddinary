About Oddinary

Oddinary is a party game of secret words, clever clues, social deduction, and imposter hunting designed for friends, family, and group gatherings.

What is Oddinary?

In Oddinary, 3 to 30 players gather around a single mobile device or desktop browser. Every player receives a secret word on their turn. Most players receive the Common Word, while one or more players receive the Odd Word (or no word at all, depending on game settings).

🕵️ Secret Roles

Each round assigns secret roles seamlessly for every player.

🔍 Clue & Investigation

Players give subtle clues about their word without spoiling it to the Imposters.

🗳️ In-App & Real Voting

Support for built-in automated scoring & voting, or real-life verbal investigation and reveal.

Game Modes

Stealth Mode (Default)

In Stealth Mode, the Imposter receives a secret word, but does not know they are the Imposter. They must blend into the game using the descriptions provided by the other players.

Classic Imposter Mode

When Stealth Mode is disabled, the Imposter is directly told that they are the IMPOSTER. They receive no word and must lie from the start to blend in with the other players.

Points Rules

When in-app voting is enabled, points are awarded based on performance:

DETECTIVES

  • +15 Points for each correctly identified Imposter.
  • 0 Points for an incorrect identification.

IMPOSTERS

  • 0 Points if caught by receiving the most votes.
  • +10 Points if suspected but successfully survived.
  • +20 Points if received zero votes.
  • Betrayal: +5 points for voting against a fellow Imposter.

Note: Imposters are considered caught if they receive the most votes or are tied for the highest count.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Oddinary free to play?

Yes! Oddinary is completely free to play directly in your web browser.

How many players can play?

Oddinary supports anywhere from 3 players up to 30 players in a single game session.

Do we need multiple devices?

No! Oddinary is designed as a pass-and-play game, requiring only one shared device passed from player to player during secret word reveal and voting.
