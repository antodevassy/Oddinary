Stealth Mode

ON: Imposters receive a secret word that is slightly different from the common word. They must blend in without realizing their word is different.

OFF: Imposters receive no word. Their card displays "IMPOSTER". They must figure out the secret word by listening to the clues.

Secret Alliance

ON: When playing with 2 or more Imposters, each Imposter will see the names of their fellow Imposter partners on their secret card.

OFF: Imposters do not know who the other Imposters are and must play independently.

Voting Mode

ON: In-app secret voting. Every player selects their suspect on the phone, and the app calculates round scores automatically.

OFF: Real-life voting. Players investigate and vote out loud out-of-app. The app reveals the Imposters directly. ⚠️ Note: Points will not be calculated when voting is OFF.

Scoring Rules

Detective
🎯 Correctly catch an Imposter +15
Imposter
😎 Survive the vote +10
👻 Receive zero votes +20
🗡️ Betray another Imposter +5
Detailed Rules:

• Imposters are considered caught if they receive the most votes or are tied for highest votes.

• If an Imposter is caught, they earn 0 points for the round.

• Betrayal: If you're an Imposter and vote against another Imposter, you earn +5 points.

• Detectives gain +15 points for every correct vote cast for an Imposter.

• Voting is OFF: Points are not calculated when voting mode is disabled.
