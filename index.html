How to Play?

Get Your Word: Everyone gets a secret word. The Imposters either get a different word or no word at all.

Describe Your Word: Take turns giving clues about your word. Don’t say the word directly. Try to figure out who the Imposters are.

Vote: Each player votes for who they think the Imposters are. You must vote for the same number of players as there are Imposters.

Score Points: Detectives get points for each correct guess. Imposters get points if they avoid being caught. Check the leaderboard to see who’s winning!

POINTS RULES

DETECTIVES

+15 Points for each correctly identified Imposter.

0 Points for an incorrect identification.

IMPOSTERS

0 Points if caught by receiving the most votes.

+10 Points if suspected but successfully survived.

+20 Points if received zero votes.

Betrayal: +5 points for voting against a fellow Imposter.

Note: Imposters are considered caught if they receive the most votes or are tied for the highest count.

Notice

Stealth Mode

ON: The Imposter receives a secret word but does not know they are the Imposter. They must blend into the game using the descriptions provided by the other players.

OFF: The Imposter is directly told that they are the IMPOSTER. They must lie from the start to blend in with the other players.

Voting

ON: Players vote in the app to find the Imposter. Scores are calculated and the leaderboard is shown.

OFF: No in-app voting. Players discuss and decide in real life. The app only reveals the Imposter at the end.
