Privacy Policy

At Oddinary, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Oddinary and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Oddinary is designed as a browser-based web party game. Game inputs, such as player names and temporary game scores, are processed locally within your browser session. We do not collect, request, or store personal user accounts or identity credentials on external server databases.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Like most website hosts, our web hosting provider follows standard procedures using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

3. Cookies & Advertising (Google AdSense)

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Oddinary.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site or other websites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or by opting out via www.aboutads.info.

Oddinary's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.
