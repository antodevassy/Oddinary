Stealth Mode

ON: Oddinaries receive a secret word that is slightly different from the common word. They must blend in without knowing their word is different.

OFF: Oddinaries receive no word. Their card displays "ODDINARY". They must bluff and guess the word from other players' clues.

Secret Alliance

ON: When playing with 2 or more Oddinaries, each Oddinary sees their fellow Oddinary partners listed on their card so they can coordinate.

OFF: Oddinaries do not know who else is an Oddinary and must operate independently.

Voting

ON: Players pass the phone to cast private secret votes. The app automatically calculates points and tracks leaderboard standings.

OFF: Verbal group voting in real life without passing the phone. Players vote out loud and tap to reveal the Oddinary directly (scores are not calculated).

SCORING RULES

DETECTIVE
Catch the Oddinary +15
Detectives earn +15 pts for each correct secret vote cast for an Oddinary.
ODDINARY
Survive the vote +10
Get zero votes +20
Betray another Oddinary +5
Oddinaries who are caught (receive the most votes or tied for highest) earn 0 points for the round.
